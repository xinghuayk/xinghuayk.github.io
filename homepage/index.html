02Research

My research asks a deceptively simple question: how do we know a model works? In high-stakes domains the answer is rarely a number on a benchmark — it is a set of practices, documents, and negotiations spread across teams who often disagree about what “working” means. I study those practices, and I build lightweight tools that help people do them better.

Evaluation in high-stakes settings

Benchmark scores travel poorly into hospitals and public agencies. I study what practitioners actually measure, how local evidence is produced, and where it fails to reach the people who approve deployments.

EvaluationField studiesClinical ML

Documentation and transparency

Model cards, datasheets, and audit reports are meant to make systems legible. I trace how these documents are written, circulated, ignored, and occasionally weaponised inside organisations.

Model cardsGovernanceOrganisations

Human–AI collaboration

When a clinician or caseworker overrides a model, that decision is data too. I design and evaluate interfaces that keep human judgement visible instead of quietly automating it away.
